AuditKIT

AuditKIT is a toolkit for auditing the safety of language models. It collects model evaluations into one reusable tool, so the same audits can be run across different models. I built it at Lexsi Labs, and it is described in an arXiv paper.

Circuit discovery for the IOI task on Llama 3.2 1B
A hand-written pipeline using layer-by-layer and token-by-token activation patching, without TransformerLens. It found a candidate circuit of 7 MLP layers and 10 attention heads out of 80 components; the necessity intervention cut the logit-difference score from 5.39 to 0.50. Comparisons with matched random baselines showed a gap between necessity and sufficiency, and the layer-level analysis reproduced across three seeds. Code
Architectural determinants of KV-cache compressibility in transformer language models
SVD-based low-rank approximations of Key/Value projections across eight language models, used to find how far the KV cache can be compressed without retraining. QK-norm and grouped-query attention let models keep only 24–35% of hidden-state width with no accuracy loss, and compressed caches transferred in agentic handoffs.

AI safety and faithfulness

Chain-of-thought faithfulness, LLM-as-a-judge, agentic safety, sycophancy, causal intervention, prompt injection.

Chain-of-thought faithfulness in financial LLMs (Lexsi Labs, ongoing)
Auditing five models by comparing internal signals (linear probes, top-k logit-lens tracking, elicited probabilities, an independent judge) with their generated explanations. I also developed and evaluated IV-DPO and CF-DPO preference-optimization pipelines to improve internal-verbal alignment.
